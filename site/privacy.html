Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Plate Log is a free gym logbook. It is run by an individual, not a company, and this version is a small test. This page explains in plain language what the app stores about you and why.

What is stored

  • Your training log: dates, sessions, exercises, weights, reps, notes, your plan, the names of any people you add inside the app, and your kg or lb choice.
  • If you create an account: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by the sign-in service and are stored scrambled, so the app owner cannot read them.
  • On your own device: the app keeps a copy of your log and settings in your browser's storage. This is how it works without an account.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to show your log, keep it in sync between your devices, sign you in, and send the confirmation and password-reset emails you ask for. Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and there are no analytics or tracking cookies.

Who handles it

  • Supabase provides sign-in and the database that holds your account log. Each account can only read and change its own rows.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr supply the fonts and the sign-in code the page loads, so they see your IP address when the page loads.

As the person who runs the app, the owner has administrator access to the database. That access is for keeping the app working, not for reading individual logs.

Without an account

If you choose Continue without an account, your log stays only in this browser. It is not uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser data erases it, so export a CSV first if you want a copy.

Your choices

  • Take a copy: use Export all sets (CSV) at the bottom of the app at any time.
  • Remove a person: removing a person inside the app deletes their logs and plan.
  • Delete your account and data: ask using the contact below and everything stored for your account will be deleted.

Children

Plate Log is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this changes in a meaningful way, the date at the top of this page will change. Paid features may be added later, and this page will be updated before that happens.

Contact

For questions or to ask for your data to be deleted, contact the person who gave you the link to Plate Log.
